Our History

Sheikh-ul-Aalam Public School was founded in 1987 in Kangan, Ganderbal, with a simple aim: to bring quality education close to home for local families. Named in honour of Sheikh-ul-Aalam, revered across Kashmir for his wisdom and humility, the school has grown over nearly four decades from a modest beginning into one of the area's most trusted institutions — now home to more than 1,500 students, with over 12,000 alumni who have passed through its classrooms since it began, while still holding on to the values it started with.

A structured academic pathway from early years through matriculation, built to grow with every student.

Secondary
